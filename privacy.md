# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

mindlink clips runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner.

## What data is used

When the owner connects an account, the tool receives access tokens and basic profile information (account name and ID) from TikTok, YouTube or Instagram. It uses them only to upload the owner's own videos to that same account and to show which account is connected.

## Where it is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official APIs of the connected platforms. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

## Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the settings of TikTok, Google or Instagram.
