# mindlink clips

mindlink clips is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and uploads short video clips made by its owner to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. On TikTok, clips are sent to the owner's inbox as drafts, which the owner reviews and posts manually in the TikTok app.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
